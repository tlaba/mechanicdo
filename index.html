Section B–B · What we do

B

Three ways we cover your maintenance

Start with one, or combine them into a single monthly agreement. No long lock-ins.

B1Detail B1

Preventive maintenance

A fixed schedule that catches wear before it costs you a shift. We build the task list around your equipment and OEM intervals, then run it on time, every time.

What’s included

  1. Equipment register and PM schedule built for your plant
  2. Lubrication, belt/chain tensioning, alignment checks
  3. Bearing, gearbox, and drive inspections
  4. Vibration and thermal checks on critical assets
  5. Photo report, findings, and parts list after each visit
Schedule
Monthly, quarterly, or shutdown-based schedules

B2Detail B2

24/7

Breakdown maintenance

When production stops, you get a millwright on the phone immediately and a crew rolling with a stocked truck. We diagnose, get you running, and tell you straight what it will take to keep it that way.

What’s included

  1. 24/7/365 dispatch — nights, weekends, holidays
  2. On-site diagnosis and emergency repair
  3. Bearing, shaft, coupling, and gearbox replacement
  4. Emergency welding, fabrication, and machining liaison
  5. Root-cause notes so the same failure doesn’t repeat
Priority
Priority response for contract customers

B3Detail B3

Shift coverage

A qualified millwright embedded on your floor for the shifts you can’t fill — vacation, medical leave, a ramp-up, or a seat you’re still hiring for.

What’s included

  1. Days, afternoons, nights, and weekend shifts
  2. Short-term fill-in or ongoing rotation coverage
  3. Shutdown and changeover surge support
  4. Works to your procedures, LOTO, and safety program
  5. Shift handover notes to your maintenance lead
Billing
Billed hourly or as a weekly block rate

G

Notes G · FAQ

G

Questions we get a lot

Line down instead? Call (647) 000-0000

Q1How fast can you get to us on a breakdown?

Contract customers in the GTA get priority dispatch, with a target of 2–4 hours on site. For new customers we’ll be straight with you on the phone about the soonest crew we can free up — often the same day.

Q2Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. Preventive maintenance runs on a simple monthly agreement you can cancel with 30 days’ notice, and breakdown or shift work can be booked purely as needed.

Q3Can you cover a night or weekend shift regularly?

Yes. Shift coverage is available on days, afternoons, nights, and weekends — as a one-off fill-in or an ongoing rotation while you recruit.

Q4Do you supply parts?

We can source and supply parts, or install parts you’ve already stocked. Either way you get the part numbers in writing so you can build a critical spares list.

Q5Are you insured?

Yes. We carry commercial general liability insurance, and we’ll send certificates and complete your contractor pre-qualification before the first visit.

Q6We have sites outside the GTA — can you still help?

Often, yes. We’re expanding through Southern Ontario and then across Canada and the US. For multi-site programs and shutdowns we already travel — send us the locations and we’ll tell you honestly what we can cover.
